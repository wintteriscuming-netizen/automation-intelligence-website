Google Ask Maps in India: How Clinics Can Get Recommended (2026 Guide)

By Rishikesh Perja · 9 November 2026 · 4 min read

If a patient asks Google Maps, "diabetes doctor near Kukatpally open on Sunday", the answer may no longer be a list of ten blue pins. It can be a short, conversational recommendation from Gemini. That feature is called Ask Maps, and it is already live in India.

This guide explains what Ask Maps is, what Google has (and hasn't) said about how it picks businesses, and a practical checklist for clinics.

What is Google Ask Maps?

Ask Maps is a chat-style feature inside Google Maps. Instead of typing a keyword, a patient can describe what they need in full sentences and ask follow-up questions. Google answers with suggestions shown on a map, and personalises them using what the person has searched or saved before.

Google announced it on 12 March 2026 and rolled it out first in the US and India, on the Android and iOS Maps apps. In August 2026 Google added Hindi support, live transit updates and more personalisation for users in India. Search Engine Journal, 9to5Google and Analytics Insight have the details.

What Google has said about how it picks businesses

Google says Ask Maps draws on its places database and reviews from contributors. It has not said how it ranks or selects which businesses appear in an answer, and it didn't announce ads at launch. So nobody outside Google can promise you a guaranteed spot, including agencies.

One related change matters for every clinic. Google confirmed in December 2025 that it is removing the public Questions & Answers section from Maps and replacing it with an AI-powered "Ask" button (Search Engine Roundtable). The old trick of seeding your own profile with questions and answers is no longer the way to control what patients see.

Why this matters for clinics

Patients ask specific questions: a specialist, a location, a language, opening hours, whether appointments are available. A conversational tool rewards businesses whose information is complete, specific and consistent. A clinic with a thin profile and a handful of "Great doctor!" reviews gives the AI very little to work with.

A practical checklist for your clinic

These steps are our recommendations based on what Google says the feature uses. They are not confirmed ranking factors.

  1. Complete every part of your Business Profile. Choose the right primary and secondary categories, list every service and specialty, and keep hours and holiday timings current.
  2. Collect detailed, genuine reviews. Reviews that mention the condition treated, the doctor and the experience give the AI more to match against a patient's question. Never offer rewards for reviews and never write them yourself, because that breaks Google's policies.
  3. Reply to reviews with care. Thank patients, but don't confirm they were your patient or mention any treatment details in public.
  4. Keep your details identical everywhere. Your name, address and phone number should match across your website, profile and directories. Duplicate listings and conflicting numbers cause confusion.
  5. Make your website answer patient questions. Create pages for each service and doctor, and add an FAQ covering fees, appointment process, languages spoken and parking. This replaces the old Q&A habit.
  6. Add fresh photos and posts. Show the clinic, the team and the facilities, and update them regularly.
  7. Test it yourself. Open Ask Maps and ask the questions your patients would ask. Note whether your clinic appears and what it says about you, then fix what's missing or wrong.

What to expect

Google's profile dashboard doesn't yet give clear data on how often a business appears in Ask Maps answers, so measure what you can: calls, direction requests and website visits. Google will likely keep changing this feature, so review your profile every month.

Frequently asked questions

What is Google Ask Maps?

Ask Maps is a conversational feature inside Google Maps, powered by Gemini. People type or speak a detailed question and get suggested places on a map, with follow-up questions possible.

Is Ask Maps available in India?

Yes. Google began rolling it out in the US and India on 12 March 2026 for the Android and iOS Maps apps, and later added Hindi support.

How do I get my clinic to appear in Ask Maps?

Google has not published how it ranks businesses. Based on what it says the feature uses, the sensible approach is a complete and accurate Business Profile, detailed genuine patient reviews, consistent details across the web, and a website that clearly describes your services.

Can I pay to appear in Ask Maps?

At launch Google did not announce ads in Ask Maps and did not say whether paid placements may come later.
