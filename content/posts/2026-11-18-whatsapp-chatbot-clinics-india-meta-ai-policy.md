If you run a clinic in Hyderabad, you may have heard that "WhatsApp banned AI chatbots." The real rule is much narrower, and appointment bots are still allowed.

## What Meta actually changed

Meta published an updated Business API policy on 18 October 2025 and it applied to all businesses from 15 January 2026. It stops general-purpose AI assistants, the ChatGPT or Perplexity style of bot, from being offered to the public through WhatsApp ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/meta-bans-third-party-ai-chatbots-like-chatgpt-and-perplexity-from-whatsapp-82837.htm)). Meta clarified that it does not cover businesses using AI to serve their own customers, such as booking, order tracking and common questions ([respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-policy-2026)).

## What a clinic can still automate

- **Appointment booking, rescheduling and cancellations**
- **Reminders** to cut no-shows
- **Answers to routine questions:** timings, address, fees, documents to bring, parking
- **Post-visit follow-up** and review requests
- **Lead capture** from the WhatsApp button on your Google Business Profile and website

## What to avoid

1. **A bot that answers open-ended medical questions.** It can be wrong, and patients may rely on it. Route clinical questions to staff.
2. **Messaging people who never opted in.** Get permission first and keep a record.
3. **Unofficial bulk-sender tools or personal numbers.** These risk getting your number blocked.
4. **Sending sensitive details carelessly.** Keep reports and diagnoses out of automated messages unless your consent and security setup supports it.

## WhatsApp Business app or API?

| | Business app | Business API (via a provider) |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | One small team replying by hand | Automation and several staff |
| Automation | Basic quick replies | Full chatbot flows and templates |
| Booking system link | No | Yes |
| Setup | Minutes | Needs an approved provider |

## A simple setup plan

1. **Pick three jobs** for the bot, such as booking, reminders and FAQs.
2. **Use the official API** through a verified provider.
3. **Write clear opt-in wording** on your website forms and Maps button.
4. **Create approved message templates** for reminders and follow-ups.
5. **Add a human handoff,** so a patient can reach a person at any time.
6. **Measure it:** bookings completed, response time and no-show rate.
