A new year is a good time to reset your local visibility. Here are 15 practical steps for Hyderabad businesses, grouped by what matters most.

## Your Google Business Profile

1. **Check every detail.** Name, address, phone, website and hours must be correct.
2. **Review your categories.** Pick the best primary category and relevant secondary ones.
3. **List all services** with short, clear descriptions.
4. **Refresh photos.** Add recent pictures of the premises, team and work.
5. **Add holiday hours** and update them when they change.

## Reviews and reputation

6. **Ask for genuine reviews** after a good experience, with a short link. Never pay for them.
7. **Reply to every review** politely, and don't reveal customer details. Our guide on [Google Ask Maps](/blog/google-ask-maps-india-clinics/) explains why detailed reviews matter more now.

## Your website

8. **Create a page for each core service** and each location you truly serve.
9. **Check speed and mobile layout.** Make the phone number and WhatsApp button easy to tap.
10. **Add schema markup** for your business, services and FAQs.
11. **Write FAQs** using the words customers use. They help both Google and AI tools.

## Messaging and data

12. **Set up WhatsApp for enquiries** with opt-in wording. See what [Meta's AI policy allows](/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-clinics-india-meta-ai-policy/).
13. **Tidy your consent and privacy wording,** ahead of India's data protection rules. Our [DPDP checklist](/blog/dpdp-act-clinics-india-checklist/) is a good start.

## Tracking

14. **Track what counts:** calls, direction requests, messages and enquiries, not just visits.
15. **Test AI search monthly.** Ask Google Maps, Gemini and ChatGPT the questions your customers ask, and fix anything wrong. For clinics, see our guide to [clinic SEO in the AI era](/blog/clinic-seo-ai-search-google-chatgpt/).
