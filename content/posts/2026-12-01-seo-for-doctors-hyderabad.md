When someone in Hyderabad types "diabetes doctor near me" or "best dermatologist in Madhapur", the clinics that appear first get the calls. This guide shows how doctors and clinics can earn those spots with local SEO.

## 1. Start with your Google Business Profile

For local searches, the profile and the map listing often matter more than your homepage.

- Choose the **right primary category** (for example, Endocrinologist or Dermatologist) and relevant secondary ones.
- List every **service** and the **languages** your team speaks.
- Keep **hours, holiday timings and phone numbers** accurate.
- Add real photos of the clinic, reception and team.
- Add a **booking or WhatsApp link** so patients can act at once.

Read our guide to [Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/gbp-optimization-cost-india/) for what a full setup includes.

## 2. Build one page per service and per doctor

A single "Services" page rarely ranks. Create a page for each important treatment ("Diabetes treatment in Hyderabad") and each doctor, with:

- a clear explanation of the condition and the treatment,
- the doctor's qualifications and experience,
- fees or what affects them, if you can share that,
- an easy way to book.

## 3. Cover your localities honestly

If you serve patients from Kukatpally, KPHB, Madhapur and nearby areas, say so, with real directions, parking and travel details for each. Don't copy the same page with only the area name changed.

## 4. Earn genuine reviews

Ask satisfied patients to share their experience, and make it easy with a short link. Never pay for reviews, never write them yourself, and reply politely without revealing patient or treatment details.

## 5. Add structured data

Schema markup helps Google understand your clinic. Use `MedicalClinic` and `Physician` markup with name, address, phone, hours and specialties, and add FAQ markup to question sections.

## 6. Make the site fast and mobile-friendly

Most patients search on a phone. Pages should load quickly, show the phone number at the top and have a tap-to-call button and a WhatsApp button.

## 7. Publish trustworthy medical content

Write patient-friendly articles on the conditions you treat. Name the doctor who wrote or reviewed each one, avoid exaggerated claims and avoid promising cures. Follow the medical advertising rules that apply to you and check current regulations.

## 8. Track what matters

Watch calls, direction requests, bookings and Search Console queries, not just traffic. Review them monthly.
