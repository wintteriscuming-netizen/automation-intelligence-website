Waking up to find your clinic or shop has vanished from Google Maps is stressful. Calls stop and patients or customers can't find you. The good news is that many suspensions can be appealed. This guide explains the common causes and a safe recovery process.

## Common reasons profiles get suspended

Google decides case by case, but these issues come up often:

- **Keywords added to the business name,** such as "Best Cheap Dentist Hyderabad" instead of the real name
- **An ineligible address,** such as a virtual office, P.O. box or a place where you have no real presence
- **Mismatched details** between your profile, website and signage
- **Editing sensitive fields** (name, address, category) after verification
- **Duplicate or unauthorised listings** for the same business
- **Fake, bought or incentivised reviews**
- **Prohibited or misleading content** in posts, photos or descriptions
- **Wrong or misleading categories**

## What to do first

1. **Read the notice.** Note exactly what Google says about the problem.
2. **Don't make panic edits** and don't create a new listing.
3. **Fix the cause.** Correct the name, address or category, or remove the violating content.
4. **Gather proof** that the business is real. Google may ask for items such as business registration or licence, a bill in the business name, signboard photos, or a short video of the premises.
5. **Submit one clear appeal** through Google's reinstatement process, with accurate details that match your website and signage.
6. **Wait for the response** and reply to any request for more information.

## What not to do

- Don't submit repeated appeals in a row.
- Don't use another business's address or fake documents.
- Don't buy reviews to look more established.
- Don't rely on anyone who guarantees reinstatement.

## How to prevent it

- Use your **real business name** as shown on your signboard.
- Keep your details **identical** across your profile, website and directories.
- Be careful before editing **name, address or category.**
- Ask for **genuine** reviews only.
- Add a **second owner or manager** you trust, so you aren't locked out.
- Keep photos of your signboard and documents ready.
