Patients no longer only type a keyword into Google. They also ask Google Maps' Ask Maps, Gemini and ChatGPT questions such as "which clinic in Kukatpally treats thyroid problems on weekends?" This guide explains what that means for your clinic.

## What has changed

Google's Ask Maps is a Gemini-powered, conversational feature that began rolling out in India and the US in March 2026 ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-maps-launches-ai-conversational-search-with-ask-maps/569585/)). We cover it in our guide to [Google Ask Maps for clinics](/blog/google-ask-maps-india-clinics/). Separate AI tools, including ChatGPT, answer local questions too, often by drawing on information published about a business across the web.

## What the data says (and doesn't)

Studies disagree. BrightEdge reported that near-me and provider-finding healthcare queries, which once triggered AI summaries, showed none in its 2025 data ([BrightEdge](https://www.brightedge.com/resources/weekly-ai-search-insights/healthcare-ai-evolution-google-2023-2025)). Other analyses show AI answers growing in other areas of search. Google changes this often, so treat any single statistic with caution and test your own searches.

## What helps AI tools recommend a clinic

These are practical recommendations, not confirmed ranking factors, and nobody can promise a mention.

1. **Keep your facts identical everywhere.** Name, address, phone, hours and specialties should match across your website, Business Profile and directories.
2. **Publish clear answers.** Write pages and FAQs in the same words patients use: "Does the clinic open on Sunday?", "What does a thyroid consultation include?"
3. **Show who is behind the content.** Add doctor names, qualifications and experience.
4. **Collect detailed, genuine reviews.** Reviews that mention the condition, the doctor and the experience give AI more to work with.
5. **Use structured data.** Mark up your clinic, doctors and FAQs.
6. **Get mentioned elsewhere.** Listings in reputable directories and local articles help confirm that you exist and are trusted.
7. **Keep content fresh.** Update timings, fees and doctor details when they change.

## How to check where you stand

Once a month, ask Google Maps, Gemini and ChatGPT the five questions your patients ask most. Record whether your clinic appears and whether the details are correct. Fix wrong information at the source.
