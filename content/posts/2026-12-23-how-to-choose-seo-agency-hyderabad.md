Choosing an SEO agency in Hyderabad is hard because most pitches sound the same. These 12 questions help you tell a serious partner from a salesperson.

## Questions about strategy

1. **How will you decide what to target?** Look for an answer based on your customers' searches, not a generic keyword list.
2. **What will you do in the first 90 days?** You should get a clear plan covering the profile, website fixes and content.
3. **Do you need access to my website, and what will you change?** A good agency explains the changes and asks permission.

## Questions about proof

4. **Can you show results from a similar business?** Ask for a real case study with figures and what was done.
5. **Which results will you report?** Calls, bookings and enquiries matter more than traffic alone.
6. **Can I speak to a current client?** Confident agencies say yes.

## Questions about how they work

7. **Who will actually do the work?** Know whether it's the person you met or a hidden junior team.
8. **How do you get reviews?** The answer should never involve buying or faking reviews.
9. **How do you handle content?** Look for original writing with accurate facts, not copied or AI-spun filler.

## Questions about money and ownership

10. **What exactly is included each month?** Get a written scope.
11. **Who owns the accounts?** You should hold owner access to your Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics, and keep everything if you leave.
12. **What is the contract length and exit process?** Avoid long lock-ins without clear deliverables.

## Red flags

- A **guaranteed first position** on Google. Nobody controls the results.
- **Very low fees** paired with big promises.
- **No written scope** or reports.
- **Secret methods,** no explanations.
- **Bought links or reviews.**

## How to decide

Ask two or three agencies the same 12 questions and compare the written answers. Then check their own website and Google Business Profile, because an agency's own visibility shows how it works.
